About

My research examines how people learn political information, when it changes their attitudes, and how long those changes last.

I also study how AI can make high-quality information more accessible to underrepresented populations, alongside questions of causal inference with noisy and high-dimensional data.

Lab affiliation: Coalas Lab (PI: Elisa Kreiss)
